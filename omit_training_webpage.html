1) The Strategic Why

Community Risk

More acquisitive offenders are likely to remain in the community. Without proactive case-building, repeat victimisation increases.

Operational Gap

IOM prioritises high harm cohorts, while CID demand remains high. OMIT closes the gap for prolific burglary and robbery offenders.

Public Confidence

Burglary and robbery drive fear and harm. Linked investigations and visible pursuit improve confidence and outcomes.

OMIT Mission: Convert intelligence into evidence and outcomes through relentless, legally robust, multi-offence investigations.

2) What We Are Asking Officers to Do

Own IOM-linked burglary/robbery idents Build linked multi-offence case files Exploit forensics (prints, DNA, footwear) Use custody as intervention point Pursue TIC opportunities
Operational expectations (click to expand)
  • Take active ownership of nominated offenders and linked offences across the BCU.
  • Create continuity between incidents to evidence patterns, MOs, and cross-scene links.
  • Prioritise footwear and print opportunities from scene to custody comparison.
  • Use every detention as an investigative trigger: checks, tasks, interview strategy, digital opportunities.
  • Work jointly with IOM coordinators, probation, custody, and forensic teams.

3) PIP2 Building Blocks to Structure Investigations

Decision Making

Use proportionate, recorded rationale at every investigative stage.

Investigation Planning

Set objectives, hypotheses, lines of enquiry, and review points.

Material Management

Capture, retain, review, and disclose material lawfully and transparently.

Interview Strategy

Plan suspect/witness strategy and evidence presentation to maximise outcomes.

Forensic Strategy

Prioritise scene-to-custody opportunities, especially footwear and fingerprint linkage.

Case File Quality

Present coherent linked offending narrative to support charging and court outcomes.

4) College of Policing APP + PACE: Mandatory Legal and Professional Baseline

This training page supports practice but does not replace current force policy, APP, legal advice, or statutory guidance.

Review requirement: Officers must review the relevant College of Policing APP on investigations, case-building, suspect and witness management, and custody-linked investigative processes.
PACE requirement: Investigative and custody decisions must be lawful, necessary, proportionate, and recorded in line with PACE and applicable Codes of Practice.
PACE reminder checklist (expand)
  • Grounds and necessity clearly documented for powers used.
  • Detention and custody decisions lawfully reviewed and auditable.
  • Interviews planned and conducted with safeguards and fairness.
  • Material handling and disclosure obligations actively managed.
  • Legal rights and entitlements explained and facilitated.

5) Quick-Use Shift Checklists

Start of Shift

  • Identify active IOM-nominated subjects.
  • Check linked burglary/robbery idents.
  • Set top 3 disruption/evidence priorities.

At Scene

  • Footwear/print strategy set and task forensic opportunities early.
  • Cross-check with known offender patterns.
  • Capture investigative opportunities for linked offending.

Custody Trigger

  • Run linked offence checks immediately.
  • Request footwear comparison where relevant.
  • Set interview strategy and digital opportunities.
