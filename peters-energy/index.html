How to find these numbers on your bill

Look for the page headed "Your electricity" or "Usage summary". With Economy 7 there are two lines, one for day and one for night.

Day units (Rate 1) · 07:00–00:00182 kWh × 32.78p
Night units (Rate 2) · 00:00–07:00121 kWh × 14.59p
Standing charge · 30 days × 54.64p£16.39
Total electricity charges, including VAT£93.70
  1. Type the day kWh and night kWh into the two boxes. If your bill only shows meter readings, take the old reading away from the new one.
  2. For cost, use the total for electricity, including the standing charge and VAT.
  3. Do the same for gas. If it shows m³ or cubic feet, use the converter below.
  4. If a bill covers two months, enter each month with half the figures.

    Price you actually paid

    Average cost per kWh, including the daily standing charge. It is higher in summer because the standing charge is spread over less use.

    For your usage

    Fixed or flexible: the short version

    Fixed

    Your unit rates and standing charges are locked for 12–24 months. Your bill still depends on how much you use, but the price of each unit doesn't change. There's usually a fee if you leave early.

    Flexible (standard variable)

    Prices follow Ofgem's price cap, which changes every three months (January, April, July, October). No exit fee. You pay less when prices fall and more when they rise.

    Economy 7 tipCheck that any quote you compare is an Economy 7 tariff with separate day and night rates, so your cheaper night units are counted.

    Before you switch

    1. Check your current deal's exit fee and end date. Most suppliers let you leave for free in the last 49 days of a fix.
    2. Compare by your yearly cost in tab 3, not the "typical household" price in adverts.
    3. Look at the standing charge as well as the unit rates.
    4. Pay by monthly Direct Debit to get the lowest prices.
    5. On the day you switch, take a day and a night meter reading and keep a photo of each.
    6. Switching takes about 5 working days. Your supply is not cut off.
    7. Never give bank details to anyone who cold-calls about switching. Go to the supplier's website yourself.
