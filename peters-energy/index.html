    Fixed or flexible: the short version

    Fixed

    Your unit rates and standing charges are locked for 12–24 months. Your bill still goes up and down with how much you use, but the price doesn't change. Usually an exit fee if you leave early.

    Flexible (standard variable)

    Prices follow Ofgem's price cap, which changes every three months (January, April, July, October). No exit fee. Cheaper when prices fall, dearer when they rise.

    Before you switch

    1. Check your current deal's exit fee and end date. Most suppliers let you leave free in the last 49 days of a fix.
    2. Compare by your yearly cost in tab 3, not the "typical household" price in adverts.
    3. Look at the standing charge too. Low users can lose out on a cheap unit rate with a high daily charge.
    4. Pay by monthly Direct Debit for the lowest prices.
    5. Take a meter reading on the day you switch and keep a photo of it.
    6. Switching takes about 5 working days and the supply is not interrupted.
    7. Never give bank details to anyone who cold-calls you about switching. Go to the supplier's website yourself.
