Golden Hour Evidence Prompts

  • Capture body‑worn video walk-through and spontaneous statements.
  • Photograph visible injuries, damaged property, and disorder in context.
  • Record first account details verbatim (time, threats, exact words).
  • Secure digital evidence: messages, call logs, tracking apps, CCTV doorbells.
  • Document children’s presentation, demeanour, and safeguarding concerns.
  • Consider neighbours / bystanders as time-sensitive witnesses.

Victim-Centred Script Prompts

  • “What are you most afraid will happen in the next 24 hours?”
  • “Has the abuse changed recently in a way that worries you?”
  • “Has strangulation, choking, or breathing restriction happened?”
  • “Are there children or dependants you are worried about right now?”
  • “Do you feel safe staying here tonight?”
