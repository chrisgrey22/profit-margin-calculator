Over the last two weeks, neighbourhood teams in Cardiff and Barry increased high-visibility patrols around transport hubs after reports of anti-social behaviour and repeat shop theft. Officers worked with local authority partners and store managers to run joint problem-solving plans, resulting in 17 arrests and a reduction in repeat incidents in the priority locations.

Victim care officers also piloted a same-day referral process for vulnerable victims linked to these incidents, improving safeguarding support and communication.
